Key facts, steps, and terms

The process generally starts with a discussion of the concern and an assessment of the roof. After that, the contractor should describe the repair and explain what work is proposed. Timing varies with the size and complexity of the repair, material needs, access, and conditions at the property. Cost is also affected by the repair's scope, materials, access, and any additional damage found. There isn't one reliable price or timeline for every roof.

A few terms you may hear:

  • Inspection: Looking over the roof or affected area to assess its condition.
  • Scope of work: The tasks and materials included in a proposed repair.
  • Flashing: Material used around roof openings and edges to help direct water away from vulnerable joints.

Ask the contractor to define any term you don't recognize and to point out which parts of the proposed scope are confirmed versus dependent on further findings.
