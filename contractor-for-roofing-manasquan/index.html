Key facts about scope, timing, and cost

Roof repair typically involves discussing the concern, assessing the roof, and explaining the recommended work. The exact steps depend on what the assessment finds. Timing can vary with the size and complexity of the job, material availability, access to the area, and conditions during the work. Cost depends on the repair scope, materials, access, and whether additional damage is identified. Ask for the factors behind a proposal rather than expecting one standard price to apply to every roof.

A few common terms can make a conversation easier:

  • Roofing material: The material used as part of the roof covering or repair.
  • Repair scope: The agreed tasks and materials for the job.
  • Leak source: The area where water is entering, which may need assessment rather than assumption based on where a mark appears indoors.

If a proposal uses unfamiliar language, ask the contractor to explain what it means for your particular repair. Confirm what is included and what would require a separate discussion.
