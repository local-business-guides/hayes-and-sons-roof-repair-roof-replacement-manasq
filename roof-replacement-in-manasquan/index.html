Key facts before planning a replacement

A replacement project starts with understanding the roof and defining the work. Don’t rely on a general timeline or cost range as a substitute for details about your property.

  • What it covers: A replacement plan should describe the roof areas included, the work involved, and the materials being considered.
  • Typical timing: Project duration depends on the job’s size, roof condition, scheduling, and other details. Ask for a timeline based on the proposed scope.
  • What affects cost: Roof size and configuration, material choices, access, and the work needed can all affect cost. Ask what the proposal includes and which conditions could add work.
