What roof repair covers and how to compare

Roof repair is work intended to address a specific problem with a roof. The exact scope can’t be determined from a search phrase alone. Share what you’ve noticed and ask the contractor to explain the proposed steps in everyday language.

Key facts to keep in mind:

  • What it covers: The specific roof concern and the repair work proposed for it. Confirm what is included and what is outside the scope.
  • Typical timing: Timing varies with the size and nature of the issue, the assessment needed, and the materials or work involved. Ask what the next steps are and when you can expect an update.
  • What affects cost: The issue’s extent, the work required, and the materials involved can affect the cost. Request an explanation of the factors rather than assuming a standard amount.
  • How to compare providers: Look for a clear scope, direct answers, and communication about uncertainties. Compare what each provider says they will address, not just broad descriptions of service.

A few terms you may hear: scope means the work included in the plan; flashing is material used at roof transitions and openings; leak source means the point where water is getting through, which may take investigation to identify.
